03 / Demo

A worked Westpac experiment — built, not pitched

Last year I designed a Westpac-specific idea and never formally presented it. So I built it. ConsentX — “Layer Trust Over Compliance” is a live working prototype that reframes youth banking onboarding as a consent journey rather than a paperwork gauntlet: life-stage data becomes trust signals, verification is automated, paperwork disappears — while auditability, parent-approved controls and revocable consent stay intact.

This is exactly the kind of time-boxed, evidence-bound, senior-executive-ready artefact The Future Lab is meant to produce — done before the conversation, not after it.

Live Prototype · ConsentX

Layer Trust Over Compliance

Youth onboarding reframed: a 14-year-old opens an account in under a minute, with full auditability, parental oversight and no regulatory shortcuts.

  • Problem, in Westpac's own terms. ~67% of under-18 applicants abandon onboarding; ~$47 compliance cost per youth account; ~$2.4B in lifetime value left on the table.
  • Target state modelled. Account opening under a minute, zero documents, automated and revocable compliance — traceable end to end.
  • Five-lens stakeholder analysis. Customer, guardian parent, Westpac innovation lead, merchant, regulator — one consent fabric, shared advantage, no compromise on safety.
  • Pilot-grade metrics surfaced. 92/100 trust score, 45-second onboarding, 70% dropout reduction, 100% compliance rate.
