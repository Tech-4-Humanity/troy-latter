03 / Prototype

A Westpac-specific idea, built

I built a Westpac-specific prototype rather than just describing one. ConsentX — “Layer Trust Over Compliance” reframes youth banking onboarding as a consent journey rather than a paperwork process: life-stage data becomes trust signals, verification is automated, while auditability, parent-approved controls and revocable consent stay intact.

To be precise about what this is: a self-built concept prototype — not a pilot, and it carries no pilot results. Its value is making the idea concrete and testable, exactly the kind of cheap, fast artefact an experimentation function should produce before a big conversation.

Concept Prototype · ConsentX

Layer Trust Over Compliance

Youth onboarding reframed as a consent journey — fast, document-light, revocable, with parental oversight and full auditability designed in from the start.

  • Problem hypothesis. Youth onboarding friction loses customers and adds compliance cost — framed as a hypothesis to test, not a stated fact.
  • Target state, modelled. Fast, document-light onboarding with automated, revocable, traceable compliance — modelled, not measured.
  • Five-lens stakeholder analysis. Customer, guardian parent, bank innovation lead, merchant and regulator — one consent fabric, no compromise on safety.
  • Honest scope. A working prototype to make the idea tangible. Any numbers shown in the demo are illustrative model inputs, not validated results.
